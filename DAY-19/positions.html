positions are 5 types they are:

  1. absolute

    2. means this is taking space from margin and use all ways
    and used all ways like top,bottom,top &right,top & left,bottom & right,bottom & left

  2. relative

    3. means taking from property space to where you want

  3. static

    4. means nothing will not change

  4. fixed

    5. fixed means that positions and you should scroll the webpage
    and used all ways like top,bottom,top &right,top & left,bottom & right,bottom & left
    it is taking that space property only(inline)

  5. sticky

    6. means fixed that position and taking whole width(block)
